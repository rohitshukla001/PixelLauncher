# User guide

How to use Pixel Launcher on your device.

## Set it as your home screen

Press the Home button and choose Pixel Launcher, or go to the system Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app and pick it. To go back, choose a different home app the same way.

## Home screen

- **Pages.** Swipe left and right between pages. The dots show which page you are on. Page 1 carries the clock and, if enabled, the search bar.
- **Add an app.** Swipe up to open the app drawer, press and hold an app, then drag it onto a home page.
- **Move an app.** Press and hold an app on the home screen and drag it where you want. Drag to the edge to move it to the next page.
- **Remove an app.** Press and hold it and drag it to the Remove area. This only clears it from the home screen; the app stays installed.
- **App options.** Press and hold an app for App Info and Uninstall.
- **Pages add themselves.** Dragging an app past the last page creates a new one, up to nine.

## The dock

The row of apps along the bottom is yours to manage with no restrictions:

- **Add:** drag an app from the drawer or a home page into the dock.
- **Rearrange:** drag an app within the dock to reorder it.
- **Remove:** drag an app out of the dock and drop it on a home page, or onto the Remove area.

## App drawer

Swipe up from the home screen.

- Apps are listed A–Z, including work-profile apps (they carry the work badge).
- The **Suggested** row shows your most-used apps.
- **Search** ignores case and accents. If nothing matches, tap "Search with …" to search the web.

## Clock and search bar

Open Settings (below) to:

- Turn the search bar on page 1 on or off.

The clock can sit on the left, centre or right.

## Wallpaper

Open Settings → Change wallpaper. Choose from:

- **Bundled photos** (nature and temple scenes),
- **16 built-in designs** drawn by the app, or
- **Your own photo**, which you can crop to fit.

If your device's administrator blocks wallpaper changes (common on managed or college-issued tablets), Pixel Launcher still shows your chosen wallpaper on its own home screen. The system lock screen and other screens may stay as the administrator set them — this is a device policy the launcher cannot override.

## Hidden apps

Open Settings → Hide Apps. The first time, you set a privacy pattern. Hidden apps disappear from the home screen, the dock, the app drawer and search. To see or unhide them, open Hide Apps again and draw your pattern. After several wrong attempts the pattern pad locks for a short time.

Your pattern is stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value — it is never kept as the pattern itself.

## Settings

Open Settings from the Pixel Launcher icon in the app drawer. From there you can change the wallpaper, hide apps and toggle the search bar.

## Privacy

Pixel Launcher has no ads, no accounts and no analytics. It uses the internet only to show web search results, and search runs in a private window that keeps no cookies or history.
